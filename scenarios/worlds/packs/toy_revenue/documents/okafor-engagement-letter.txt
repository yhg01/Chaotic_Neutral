MARLOW AND FINCH
14 Corn Street, Wexley

Mr Daniel Okafor
By hand

Dear Mr Okafor,

Thank you for asking Marlow and Finch to look after your tax affairs. This letter
sets out what we will do for you.

We will prepare your yearly income return from the records you give us, file it
with the revenue office before it is due, and tell you what tax is payable. We will
act as your tax agent, so the revenue office may write to us about your return.

Tom Finch will do the work on your file. I supervise it, and you may speak to
either of us. We will not file a return until you have seen the figures and
agreed them. You remain responsible for the records being complete and correct.

Our fee for the yearly return is fixed and is set out in the fee note enclosed.

Please sign and return the copy of this letter to show that you agree.

Yours sincerely,

Nadia Marlow
Marlow and Finch

Signed and agreed: D. Okafor
